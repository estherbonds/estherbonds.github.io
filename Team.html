Jay W. Shin

About

Jay Shin obtained his BA in Computer Science from Boston University and his Ph.D. from ETH Zürich. He joined the Genome Institute of Singapore (GIS) in 2022 after fifteen-years at RIKEN (Yokohama, Japan). He is also an Adjunct Associate Professor at the National University of Singapore (NUS).

He is the co-leader of the FANTOM6 consortium and an organizing committee member of the Human Cell Atlas.
